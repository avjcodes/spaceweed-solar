About Space Weed

We got our name from our early supporters, who compared the texture of our Flash Frozen bud to astronaut ice cream. Space Weed is one of the only truly different cannabis products on the market.

Our story

Drying the old way was slow and unpredictable. We didn’t have the space, and flower lost quality before it reached the shelf. So we experimented with temperature, pressure and timing until we could freeze the flower at harvest and dry it under vacuum. That became Flash Frozen Cured, and Space Weed is the flower-only brand built on it.

CannaGenesis, the parent company

CannaGenesis is the vision of Eric Young. We’re a lot more than a cannabis company: we use science to change how cannabis is made. Cannabis defined itself in the era of prohibition, as part of the counter-culture. Those days are gone, but much of how cannabis is produced and consumed still runs on old techniques.

We believe science should lead.

We run focus groups and research to develop new techniques like Flash Frozen curing.
